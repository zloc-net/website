Know where everything is, as it moves.

zLoc tracks people, robots and goods indoors in real time. Anchors on the walls, a small tag on whatever moves, and one box on your network that hands every position to your own software.

Talk to us

What you get

Live positions

Every tag's position, speed and whether it is standing still, five times a second — up to twenty where things move fast.

Events and alarms

Zone entries and exits, an alarm when someone steps into a restricted area, jumps for sport.

Session reports

Distance, speeds, jumps and time in each zone per person, built from the tag's own recording so nothing lost on the radio is missing.

Your data, on your network

The positions are computed and served in the room. Nothing has to leave your site.

Built for

Sports

Players on the court

Distance, sprints, jumps and time on court, live on the bench and in a report after training.

Robots

Machines that need to know where they are

Fast, steady position updates with speed, and geofences a robot must not cross.

Warehouses

Goods and people on the floor

Where each pallet is, who is in which area, and an alarm when someone enters a machine zone.
